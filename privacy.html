Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

TraffiAlert GH (“the app”, “we”) helps drivers in Ghana get camera and traffic alerts. This policy explains what data we use and why.

Who is responsible for your data: TraffiAlert GH is operated by Medit USA LTD, which controls the personal data described in this policy. Contact: traffialertgh@gmail.com.

Location

The app uses your device’s location on the device to show nearby cameras, speed limits, map features, and alerts while you drive. Location is processed locally for these alerts, except the route points described under “Where to? and directions” and the approximate area described under “Anonymous usage stats”. We do not sell your location data.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not sell your location history.
  • We do not use your data for advertising networks (today’s free app).

Data we may collect later

If you opt into shared camera or traffic reports (via our backend), those reports may include the approximate location of a hazard you report. You can choose not to use that feature.

Where to? and directions

When you search for a place or plan a route, the app sends what you typed, your starting point and the destination to free OpenStreetMap search and route services so they can find the place and plan the route. If you leave the route during a trip, your current position and direction are sent so a new route can be planned. These requests do not include your name, phone number or device id, and TraffiAlert does not keep them.

Anonymous usage stats

To count how many people use TraffiAlert and roughly where, the app sends a scrambled install code (made from the random device id, and it cannot be turned back into it), the app version and the phone type while the app is open. We also note the country your connection comes from. Once a day, if you have already allowed location, the app sends your approximate area, rounded to about 11 km, which we match to the nearest town and region. We never keep your exact position, your route or your internet address for these stats, and we only look at totals per area. Area records are deleted after 35 days.

Payments and Premium

If you buy Premium on traffialert.com, the payment is collected by Medit USA LTD through Paystack. You enter your Mobile Money or card details in Paystack’s secure payment window. We never see or store your card number or MoMo PIN. Paystack’s own privacy policy applies to the details you give them.

To give you your Premium code and to help if something goes wrong, we keep: the plan you bought, the amount, the date, the payment method type (for example Mobile Money), the payment number (starting with TA-), the phone number you typed on our page, your email if you chose to give one, and your Premium code. When you activate the code in the app, we link it to the same scrambled install code described under “Anonymous usage stats” so the code works on one phone. We keep payment records as long as needed for your Premium, refunds and the law on business records. We do not use them for advertising and we do not sell them.

Service providers we use

We use these outside services to run the app and this website. Each one only gets what it needs for its job, and their own privacy terms also apply:

  • Supabase — our database. It stores the anonymous usage stats described above and which scrambled install code a Premium code is activated on.
  • Render — runs our payment service, which starts payments with Paystack and gives out Premium codes.
  • Paystack — collects Premium payments (Mobile Money or card) for Medit USA LTD.
  • GitHub — hosts this website and the Android app downloads (APK files). When you visit the site or download the app, GitHub receives your internet address and browser details. The home page also asks GitHub for the latest app version number.
  • Google Fonts — the fonts on this website are loaded from Google, which receives your internet address and browser details when a page loads.
  • OpenStreetMap services and map providers — place search, route planning, map tiles and open map data, as described under “Where to? and directions”.

Some of these providers handle data outside Ghana, for example in the United States.

How long we keep data

  • Approximate area records for usage stats: deleted after 35 days.
  • Other usage stats (scrambled install code, app version, phone type, country): kept only as long as we need them to count how many people use the app.
  • Payment records and Premium codes: as long as needed for your Premium, refunds and the law on business records (see “Payments and Premium”).
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to answer you and keep a record of support and refund requests.
  • Place searches and route requests: not kept by TraffiAlert.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing traffialertgh@gmail.com. Usage stats do not include your name, so to find payment records please include your payment number (starting with TA-) and the phone number you paid with. We may need to keep some payment records where the law requires it.

You can stop the approximate area being shared by not allowing location for the app, and you can stop all sharing by uninstalling it.

Ghana data protection law

We handle personal data in line with Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). If you are not happy with how we handled your data or your request, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.
