FAQ

Quick answers — or ask Maame Serwaa.

Is TraffiAlert GH free?

Yes. The Android app is free to download and use. Premium and Founding Driver options are coming later (prices shown above) and are clearly marked as coming soon.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones. Download the arm64 APK for most modern phones, or the 32-bit version for older devices. iOS is coming soon.

Is it safe / legal to use while driving?

Alerts are voice-first and hands-free so you can keep your eyes on the road. Always follow Ghana road traffic laws and never interact with the screen while driving. TraffiAlert helps you notice cameras and limits — it does not encourage speeding.

Does it use a lot of data?

Location is processed on your device for alerts. Map tiles may use a small amount of mobile data when the map is open. Camera alert data is designed to work efficiently on Ghana networks.

When is iOS coming?

iOS is on the roadmap after the Android app is solid on the Play Store path. Follow @TraffiAlertGh on socials for updates.

How do I install the APK?

Tap Download, open the file, and allow installs from this browser / unknown sources when Android prompts you. This is normal for apps installed outside the Play Store.
